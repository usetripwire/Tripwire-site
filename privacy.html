The short version

This website has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. Your wallet is only needed for Radar, and we don't store the addresses you scan.

What happens when you scan

When you scan a token, its address is sent to our scan server, which reads public data from our Solana RPC provider and DexScreener. If our server is unavailable, your browser sends the address directly to:

  • our Solana RPC provider, [PROVIDER], to read the token's on-chain settings and largest holders;
  • DexScreener, to read its trading pools and market data;
  • Google Fonts, which serves this site's typefaces when the page loads.

Like any website, those services and our host, [HOST], receive standard request data such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies. The token address you scan is added to the page link so you can share results. It stays in your browser history unless you share it.

Radar sign-in

If you connect a wallet to unlock Radar, we receive your public wallet address and a signature proving you own it, and we check its $TRIP balance on the public blockchain. We never get access to your funds, and we never ask you to approve a transaction. Your sign-in is kept in your own browser for up to 24 hours, and we don't link your wallet to anything else about you.
